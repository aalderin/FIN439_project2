# Costco (COST) Buffett-Style Memo

## Verdict: Own the business

Costco shows the kind of durable business economics I would want to own. ROE remained above 15% in every reported fiscal year: 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%. Net income rose from $5,844 million in 2022 to $9,226 million in 2026.

Owner earnings were positive in all five years. They increased from $3,501 million in 2022 to $9,382 million in 2026, even though they dipped from $6,745 million in 2023 to $6,629 million in 2024. Revenue also rose from $226,954 million to $303,154 million over the period.

The three code-based checks pass: durable ROE, positive owner earnings, and growth in owner earnings from the first year to the last. Based on these operating results, I would own the business. This memo judges business quality only; it does not judge whether the current market price is attractive.
